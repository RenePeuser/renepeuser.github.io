After more than 20 years of building software — from PLC systems and VB6 user interfaces to reusable
.NET platforms with millions of NuGet downloads — I've collected a lot of experience. Most of it lives
in my head, in old repositories, or in slides from conference talks.

This blog is where I'll write it down.

## What to expect

- **Framework & platform engineering** — how to build abstractions teams actually want to use
- **Testing** — integration tests, snapshots, contract tests and why _testing is architecture_
- **ASP.NET Core & Minimal APIs** — validation, Problem Details, OpenAPI, versioning
- **Roslyn & tooling** — analyzers, code fixes, CLI tools and automation
- **Lessons learned** — the things I wish someone had told me earlier

## The guiding idea

> Build once. Reuse everywhere. Automate everything.

If a concern is stable, cross-cutting and repeated, solve it deliberately — once — and let product
teams focus on their domain.

See you in the next post. 👋
